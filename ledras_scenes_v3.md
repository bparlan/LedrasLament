# Ledras Lament Scenes (Version 3)

## 1. Intro
- **Visuals**: Smoke and fog over an amphitheater.
- **Background**: Sand dunes under a full moon, ancient destroyed city remains.

## 2. Ritual
- **Setting**: Full moon night.
- **Effects**:
  - *Andoveo*: Letters appear and move downwards.
  - Dark red sparkles circle around the lying body.

## 3. Astarte Awakenes
- **Transition**: Full moon transforms into a red blood moon.
- **Environment**: Sand, yellow stone.

## 4. Susta
- **Scene**: Night desert sand gradually gives way to greenery and flourishing trees.

## 5. Dialogue
- **Elements**: Introduction of a water element.
- **Animation**: Trees and crops sway with the wind; it's springtime, but set at night.

## 6. Lullaby
- **Elements**: Water element persists, with increasingly abundant trees and greenery.

## 7. Ether Walk
- **Focus**: Smoke and wind elements dominate the stairs and the top white screen.

## 8. Drag Queen
- **Atmosphere**: Calm nighttime in the village.

## 9. March of Mourn
- **Visuals**: Stairs burn, fire element present.
- **Celestial**: Blood moon returns on the white screen, which is also on fire.
