# Image-Gen Prompts — Choreographer Stage Flow

> [!info] Scope note
> This follows the choreographer's stage list exactly, in order. It replaces the earlier "12 Visual Concept Scenes" doc as the working sequence — that file's motifs (Eteocypriot script, Astarte→Aphrodite, water/green arc) are carried over as texture, but the **beat structure below is authoritative**.
>
> All prompts are single still frames — no motion, no animation language. Consecutive prompts within a stage act as keyframes for the visual delta described, for you to storyboard the transition yourself.

**Aesthetic guardrail (applies to every prompt below):** Cypro-Phoenician / Levantine Bronze Age ruin style — ashlar sandstone, mudbrick, weathered limestone blocks, no Doric/Ionic columns, no white Pentelic marble, no Parthenon-silhouette structures. Think Enkomi/Kition-era Cyprus, not Athens.

**Surfaces referenced throughout:** Ground/sand (desert floor), Stairs (amphitheater steps), Top white screen (rear projection surface), Centre (amphitheater floor — kept as negative space/dancer focus in several stages).

---

## 8:00–8:30 — Installation

**Prompt:**
"Wide desert landscape at night under a full moon, rolling sand dunes, the broken remains of an ancient Cypro-Phoenician city half-buried in sand — collapsed ashlar sandstone blocks, weathered mudbrick walls, no columns, no white marble, asymmetrical ruin silhouette. Cold moonlight, long soft shadows across the dunes, still and empty, no figures, wide static establishing shot, painterly realism."

## Ritual

### R1 — Base state (chant vibrations begin)

**Prompt:**
"Same desert ruin under full moon as the installation scene, now with faint concentric rings of pale silver light frozen mid-emanation from the ground at the ritual circle, suggesting vibration without motion blur, subtle warm glow beneath the cold moonlight, otherwise still and undisturbed."

### R2 — 22nd Boom: first letter

**Prompt:**
"A large blank white projection screen at night, a single glowing angular Eteocypriot-style glyph rendered top-right corner of the screen, pale gold light against stark white, minimal, sharp-edged, ancient syllabary aesthetic, high contrast, nothing else on the screen."

### R3 — 24th Boom: second letter

**Prompt:**
"Same blank white projection screen, two glowing angular Eteocypriot-style glyphs positioned right-to-left starting from the top-right corner, pale gold light against stark white, minimal, sharp-edged ancient syllabary aesthetic, high contrast."

### R4 — 26th Boom: third letter

**Prompt:**
"Same blank white projection screen, three glowing angular Eteocypriot-style glyphs arranged right-to-left in a single row from the top-right corner, pale gold light against stark white, minimal, sharp-edged ancient syllabary aesthetic, high contrast."

### R5 — 28th Boom: fourth letter (complete line, RTL)

**Prompt:**
"Same blank white projection screen, four glowing angular Eteocypriot-style glyphs arranged in a complete right-to-left row, pale gold light against stark white, minimal, sharp-edged ancient syllabary aesthetic, high contrast, the full four-glyph sentence clearly readable as a unit."

### R6 — Sentence travels to Astarte / red sparkles circle her body

**Prompt:**
"An ancient reclining goddess figure lying on weathered sandstone within desert ruins at night, carved in a Phoenician/Near Eastern votive style — not Greek marble, more angular terracotta-and-stone figurine aesthetic — dark blood-red sparks arranged in a circular halo pattern around her reclining body, four glowing Eteocypriot glyphs hovering just above her chest as if just arrived, full moon overhead, dark and reverent atmosphere."

## Astarte Awakens

**Prompt:**
"The same desert ruins and reclining Phoenician-style goddess figure, but the sky now holds a deep blood-red full moon instead of a pale one, red moonlight washing over the sand and broken stone, the goddess figure's surface catching a faint red glow, ominous and charged stillness, no other light sources."

## Sousta

### S1 — Early state (dancers below, stairs beginning to green, centre empty)

**Prompt:**
"An ancient stone amphitheater seen from the empty centre floor looking up at the stairs, night desert sand covering the ground at the base where a ritual circle is implied but unoccupied by any rendered figures, small sparse green shoots and thin young saplings just visible pushing up between the amphitheater's stone steps, blood-red moon in the sky above, centre of the amphitheater floor left empty and dark."

### S2 — Developed state (fuller trees higher up the stairs, centre still empty)

**Prompt:**
"Same ancient stone amphitheater viewed from the empty centre floor, now with fuller, taller trees and dense greenery growing directly out of the stone steps rising toward the top of the amphitheater, more coverage near the upper tiers than the lower ones, blood-red moon above, the centre of the amphitheater floor still bare and empty, dramatic scale contrast between bare centre and green stairs."

## Dialogue between Astarte and Ayda

**Prompt:**
"Amphitheater stairs at night covered in trees and crops bent sideways as if caught mid-wind, night-time Spring atmosphere despite the darkness, a thin sheet of water catching moonlight at the base of the stairs, blood-red moon transitioning back toward pale silver in the sky, cool night air quality, centre of the amphitheater kept dark and undefined."

## Lullaby

**Prompt:**
"Amphitheater stairs and the top white rear screen both dense with abundant flourishing greenery and trees, a calm sheet of water reflecting pale moonlight across the lower steps, the centre of the amphitheater floor rendered dark, minimal, and empty in contrast to the lush stairs and screen, soft still night atmosphere, negative space reserved at centre."

---

## Ether Walk

**Prompt:**
"Amphitheater stairs and the top white rear screen filled with soft drifting smoke and a suggestion of wind-caught particles frozen in place, pale moonlight diffused through the smoke layer, the centre of the amphitheater floor left dark, open, and empty to hold visual focus, ethereal and hazy atmosphere concentrated on the stairs and screen only."

## Drag queens

**Prompt:**
"A calm, quiet Cypriot village square at night, low stone-and-mudbrick houses, warm lantern light in a few windows, empty cobbled street, soft moonlight, peaceful and still, no ruins, no ritual elements, a gentle domestic night-time atmosphere distinct from the desert/ritual scenes."

## March of Mourn

**Prompt:**
"Amphitheater stairs engulfed in fire, flames climbing the stone steps, thick orange and red firelight replacing the earlier greenery, the top white rear screen also shows a blood-red full moon that appears to be on fire, flame textures overlaid on the screen's moon image, intense heat-red and black smoke palette, the centre of the amphitheater floor lit by the fire's glow but still empty."
