# 12 Visual Concept Scenes — Projection Mapping

Arc logic: the 12 scenes trace the show's stated emotional structure — **grief/division → memory → transformation → renewal/hope** — and are built around three recurring visual pillars from the source material: (1) water → greening, (2) concrete/division fracturing the ancient garden city, (3) Astarte → Aphrodite as the guiding symbolic thread. Mapping surface throughout: amphitheater steps/tiers as primary canvas, with a secondary rear/backdrop screen where noted.

Each scene includes a **generation prompt** line — usable directly in an image/video model.

---

## Act I — Lament (Grief, Division, Loss)

### Scene 1 — Invocation: An Do Ve O

**Placement:** Opening
**Visual:** Total darkness. A single point of warm light pulses at the center of the amphitheater floor like a heartbeat, in rhythm with a spoken/sung "An Do Ve O" ("come"). Faint Eteocypriot glyphs flicker into visibility at the light's edge, then vanish before they can be read.
**Motion:** Slow pulse, glyphs appear/dissolve asynchronously, no camera movement.
**Palette:** Black, single amber-gold light source.
**Generation prompt:** _"Ancient stone amphitheater at night, total darkness except a single pulsing warm amber light at center, faint undeciphered ancient glyphs flickering at the edge of the light and dissolving, minimal, ritualistic, cinematic, high contrast."_

### Scene 2 — The City of Gardens (Ledra, before)

**Placement:** Early Act I — historical establishing image
**Visual:** An aerial-feeling rendering of ancient Ledra: terraced gardens, open irrigation channels, dense greenery threaded through low stone architecture. Water visibly moves through channels toward the amphitheater itself, as if the theater sits inside the garden.
**Motion:** Slow push-in; water in the channels animates continuously.
**Palette:** Ochre stone, deep green, silver water.
**Generation prompt:** _"Ancient Mediterranean garden city, terraced green gardens with stone irrigation channels carrying flowing water, low ochre-stone architecture, lush and abundant, golden-hour light, painterly historical illustration style."_

### Scene 3 — Concrete Encroachment

**Placement:** Direct cut/dissolve from Scene 2
**Visual:** The same garden city is progressively overtaken by grey concrete slabs rising from the ground, sealing over the channels and soil. Green recedes to the margins of frame until only ~3% remains visible — a literal visualization of the stat.
**Motion:** Time-lapse style overtaking, concrete "grows" upward and outward from the center.
**Palette:** Shift from green/ochre to flat grey, water disappearing.
**Generation prompt:** _"Time-lapse of grey concrete slabs spreading and rising over a green garden city, water channels being sealed shut, vegetation shrinking to small isolated patches, desaturating color grade, oppressive urban expansion."_

### Scene 4 — The Line of Division

**Placement:** Mid Act I
**Visual:** A jagged fracture line splits the projected cityscape in two, rendered like a scar/buffer zone — barbed, rusted, overgrown with weeds on both sides but connecting to nothing. Fragments of the same building appear on both sides of the crack, unable to reunite.
**Motion:** The crack slowly widens, then holds static as a tension beat.
**Palette:** Rust, grey, weed-green, harsh shadow.
**Generation prompt:** _"A jagged dividing line splitting a Mediterranean city map in two, rusted barrier aesthetic overgrown with weeds, mirrored architectural fragments on either side unable to reconnect, tense and desolate, high contrast graphic style."_

## Act II — Memory (Archive, Ancestors, Undeciphered Language)

### Scene 5 — Archive of Memory

**Placement:** Transition into Act II
**Visual:** Sepia-toned archival photographs and etchings of old Nicosia/Ledra dissolve into one another — faces, market scenes, garden courtyards — as if pages of a photo album turning in the wind. Text fragments in Eteocypriot script drift across the images without resolving into meaning.
**Motion:** Slow crossfade cycle, drifting particles/dust motes.
**Palette:** Sepia, faded gold, soft grain.
**Generation prompt:** _"Sepia archival photographs of an old Mediterranean city dissolving into one another like turning album pages, faint ancient undeciphered script drifting across the images, dust motes in warm light, nostalgic documentary texture."_

### Scene 6 — Eteocypriot Script Field

**Placement:** Act II, ritual interlude
**Visual:** Full-surface projection of Eteocypriot alphabet characters flowing across the amphitheater steps like water, glyphs constantly forming and breaking apart — legible as a system, illegible as language. Performers standing on the steps become partially covered in the flowing text.
**Motion:** Continuous horizontal flow, glyphs ripple over uneven stone surface (mapped to actual step geometry).
**Palette:** Pale blue-white script on dark stone.
**Generation prompt:** _"Ancient undeciphered alphabet characters flowing like liquid across stone amphitheater steps, glyphs forming and dissolving continuously, pale luminous script on dark stone, abstract and hypnotic, projection-mapping texture."_

### Scene 7 — Ancestors Reclaim the Stage

**Placement:** Act II climax — matches the staging inversion concept
**Visual:** Silhouettes of ancestral figures rise from within the amphitheater's own seating tiers (not the stage floor), as if the stone itself is releasing them. They face outward toward where the living audience stands. The image should reinforce that the seats are the stage.
**Motion:** Figures emerge gradually from the stone texture, rising to full height, then hold.
**Palette:** Warm stone gold, figures rendered as darker silhouette or faint golden outline.
**Generation prompt:** _"Silhouetted ancestral figures slowly emerging from within ancient stone amphitheater seating, as if rising out of the stone itself, facing outward toward the viewer, golden backlight, ritualistic and monumental, mapped to stepped stone geometry."_

## Act III — Transformation (Astarte → Aphrodite, Grief Spiral, Renewal)

### Scene 8 — Call to Astarte

**Placement:** Act III opening
**Visual:** A Mesopotamian-styled goddess figure, rendered in relief/bas-relief style (not photoreal), appears faintly at the center, surrounded by concentric ring patterns associated with the Astarte/Venus cult iconography. Doves and star/rosette symbols orbit slowly.
**Motion:** Slow rotation of orbiting symbols, figure remains still and central.
**Palette:** Deep indigo background, gold relief lines.
**Generation prompt:** <br>_"Ancient Mesopotamian goddess figure rendered as glowing gold bas-relief on dark indigo background, surrounded by slowly orbiting doves and rosette star symbols, ancient religious iconography, symmetrical and reverent, sacred geometry."_

### Scene 9 — Grief Spiral

**Placement:** Act III, the "depression spiral" beat
**Visual:** A dark vortex/spiral forms at the center of the amphitheater floor, pulling fragmented imagery inward — pieces of the concrete city, the dividing line, sepia photographs — all being drawn down and consumed by the spiral. Surreal, disorienting.
**Motion:** Continuous inward spiral rotation, accelerating slightly toward the midpoint of the scene.
<br>**Palette:** Near-black with fragments of desaturated color being swallowed.
**Generation prompt:** _"Dark swirling vortex on the ground pulling in fragmented images of concrete buildings, dividing walls, and old sepia photographs, surreal and disorienting, high contrast black background, cinematic spiral motion."_

### Scene 10 — Astarte Becomes Aphrodite

**Placement:** Act III turning point
**Visual:** The bas-relief goddess from Scene 8 slowly transforms — the rigid Mesopotamian relief style softens and blooms into a more organic, Hellenic Aphrodite form emerging from sea-foam/water motifs, echoing the "water returns" theme. This is the visual hinge from lament to hope.
**Motion:** Morph/dissolve transformation, water/foam particles building the new form.
**Palette:** Shift from indigo/gold to sea-blue, white foam, warm rose light.
**Generation prompt:** _"Ancient bas-relief goddess figure dissolving and transforming into a classical Aphrodite emerging from sea foam and flowing water, morphing style from rigid gold relief to soft flowing marble and water, ethereal transformation, warm rose and sea-blue light."_

## Act IV — Renewal (Water Returns, Hope, Shared Future)

### Scene 11 — Water Returns / The City Greens

**Placement:** Act IV, mirrors Scene 3 in reverse
**Visual:** Direct visual inversion of Scene 3: water floods back into the sealed channels, cracking the concrete from beneath. Green shoots push up through the fractures. The amphitheater steps themselves appear to "green" as the projection climbs the stone.
**Motion:** Water flows outward from center, concrete cracks progressively, greenery grows upward through the cracks in real time.
**Palette:** Grey concrete giving way to vivid green and clear water — the most saturated, hopeful palette in the piece.
**Generation prompt:** _"Water flooding back through cracks in grey concrete, green plant shoots bursting up through the fractures and spreading, concrete transforming into fertile garden ground, vivid hopeful color grade, time-lapse growth."_

### Scene 12 — Shared Future / Night Sky and Birds

**Placement:** Closing
**Visual:** The amphitheater dissolves into open night sky. Flocks of birds rise from the stone tiers and fly outward over the heads of the audience standing below, symbolically crossing the performer/audience line established in Scene 7. Stars slowly resolve into a faint constellation that echoes the Eteocypriot script shapes from Scene 6 — language and sky merging as one open, unresolved, hopeful field.
**Motion:** Birds rise and disperse outward past the "camera"/audience position; stars twinkle and slowly connect into constellation lines.
**Palette:** Deep night blue, silver stars, dark bird silhouettes.
**Generation prompt:** _"Flocks of birds rising from ancient stone amphitheater steps and flying outward over the viewer into a deep night sky, stars slowly connecting into constellation lines that echo an ancient alphabet's shapes, open and hopeful, wide cinematic night sky."_

## Cross-Scene Technical Notes

- **Mapping surface priority:** Scenes 1, 6, 7, 11, 12 are written to use the actual stepped geometry of the amphitheater as canvas (not a flat screen) — these need the highest-fidelity site scan/measurements once the venue visit happens.
- **Recurring symbol system:** Eteocypriot glyphs (Scenes 1, 5, 6, 12) should be built as one reusable asset library, not regenerated per-scene, so they read as a consistent "unreadable language" across the show rather than random text.
- **Color arc:** Palette should move black/amber (1) → ochre/green (2) → grey (3–4) → sepia (5) → blue-white (6) → gold (7–8) → black-fragmented (9) → rose/sea-blue (10) → vivid green (11) → night-blue (12). This is a usable reference for the light designer to sync LX cues to projection beats.
- **2 projection units (confirmed in notes):** likely split as (a) main amphitheater-step mapping and (b) rear/sky backdrop — Scenes 2–4, 9 suit a flat backdrop; Scenes 1, 6, 7, 11 suit step-mapping; Scene 12 likely needs both units simultaneously (steps + sky backdrop).
